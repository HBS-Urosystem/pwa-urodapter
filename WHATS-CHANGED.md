# What's Changed

Differences between the original scraped site (`scraped/app.urodapter.com/`) and this app.

---

## Branding

The app **follows the device light/dark setting automatically**. **Light** appearance is based on the **Urodapter brandbook**; **dark** appearance is based on the **Urosystem brandbook**.

All text, headings included, uses the **Source Sans 3** typeface. The font files are served by the app itself (and cached for offline use), so no request goes to Google Fonts and visitors' IP addresses are not sent to Google.

## New Features (not in original)

- **PWA:** Installable app, offline use, service worker, and manifest. The service worker is not installed on local development hosts (`localhost`, `*.localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `::1`, any port); there a previously installed one removes itself and its caches.
- **Offline video:** Instruction videos can be cached; optional **download all** on the home page for full offline viewing and fewer repeat downloads on poor connections.
- **Install guide:** `/install` with steps per platform.
- **Doctor steps:** The page is titled **Instillation by a clinician**. **Step 1 of both the female and the male instructions is _What to do prior to the instillation_** (the former doctor tab of that page: points A, B, C, with the _Emptying the bladder_ dialog; the disinfection dialogs are not shown). Numbered **step circles** under the Female / Male tabs open any step directly (the same size as the self-instillation step circles, spread over the full width; the row scrolls sideways only when the circles do not fit, the edge of the row fades out where more circles are hidden, and the last circle stays in view), and the step heading carries its number. **Every step has its own address** (e.g. `/instructions-for-doctors-on-female-patients/spooning`), so a step can be linked; the address without a step opens the step last viewed. The **bottom bar steps through the instructions** with the number and title of the previous / next step (before the first step it leads to the _Table of Contents_, after the last step to the _FAQ_). **After the last step of every step sequence there is a page of real-case videos** (recordings of real instillations: three female patients after the female clinician steps and the three self-instillation approaches, one male patient after the male clinician steps); its circle and badge show a **play icon** instead of a number; before a video is started its picture is blurred and carries a play button and the warning "Explicit medical content · 18+", and the whole picture starts the video, replacing the back / next / start over buttons inside the card; a **Start at step 1** button appears below the card from step 2 on. The card is as tall as the current step. Changing the step in any way (circle, bottom bar, arrow keys, swipe) leaves the top of the card visible, and the browser's Back button leaves the instructions in one go instead of walking back through the steps. Each instruction video shows a large **play button** over the picture while it is paused, so the whole picture starts the video (the same applies to the videos of the self-instillation pages; the introductory video page keeps the standard player). Carousel remembers **which step** you were on after reload; **arrow keys** and swipe; **reduced motion** respected where relevant.
- **Updates:** Users are **notified** with a Refresh button only when the app they are running is **not the latest deployed version** (e.g. an installed PWA resumed from the background). If the update was installed automatically and the app already loaded the fresh version, no button is shown.
- **Share:** Share action for distributing the app (e.g. in clinics).
- **SEO:** Per-page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, **Open Graph / Twitter** previews, **sitemap** and **robots.txt**.
- **Accessibility:** Better structure and keyboard use on key flows (e.g. instruction carousel).
- **Self-instillation at home:** guidance for bladder instillation at home in three approaches – lying or semi-reclined, standing in the shower, partner-assisted. The three approaches are **three tabs of one page** (`/self-instillation`), each with its own address; the introductory text ("Read first") is a section above the tabs that opens on request (open on the first visit; when it closes because the reader chose a tab, the page scrolls to the line that asks the reader to choose an approach), and the page opens the approach last viewed. Each approach has a picture (from 640px wide it stands in the approach's tab above the tab label; when the tabs are held under the header, the pictures slide under the header and only the labels stay in view), a **short summary** (8 numbered steps, then a row with a play icon that opens the real-case videos) and **detailed instructions** with one page per step (step circles 1–8 with a list icon “0” back to the short summary, previous / next bar, breadcrumb; moving from step to step does not add entries to the browser history, so the Back button returns to the summary in one go). The anatomical figure opens on request, and selecting it shows it full screen. In all three approaches, steps 5–8 also show clinician instruction videos that open on request the same way (in step 6 the sealing video replaces the sealing drawing); the video in step 7 is titled “Instilling the solution”; in step 8 the leakage video section lists the three lettered checks (A–C) under the video and plays a version shortened to its first 14 seconds (the clinician manual keeps the full video), and the two other videos are titled “Relaxing the sphincter” and “Adjusting the angle”; the step 8 text is the same in all three approaches, in the partner-assisted approach worded for the partner who holds the syringe; the app remembers the last opened step per approach (“Continue at step n”). Changing the step (bottom bar or step circle) keeps the scroll position, then scrolls smoothly up to the title of the new step when the title is out of view (to the top of the page when the new step is shorter than the window). All patient pages are prerendered and work offline after the first visit.
- **Camera mirror:** every "mirror" in a heading is a button that opens the front camera as a full-screen lighted mirror: the live picture, mirrored; magnification up to 5× (pinch, mouse wheel or trackpad, `+` / `-`); dragging or the arrow keys move the magnified picture; a white light frame around the edge of the screen (off / soft / bright; starts at soft); the screen stays on. Until the page is reloaded, the mirror opens again with the light, magnification and position last chosen. Exit, Escape or the browser's Back closes it and returns to the same place on the page. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

## Privacy Policy Changes

- Removed irrelevant third-party list (Facebook, Instagram, PayPal, Pinterest, Playbuzz, Viber, Vimeo, YouTube) from the data transfer section
- Added **Netlify** as the sole external data processor (hosting, server logs, form submissions and their spam filtering); fonts, images and videos are served by the app itself
- Added "Last Updated" date
- Added the Data Controller's postal address, e-mail and company registration number
- Every visitor is covered: the server logs (IP address and request data) are listed, not only contact form data
- Messages are covered only when sent through the contact form; e-mail is no longer named as a way of sending them (the Controller's e-mail address remains for exercising data subject rights)
- Health data section: the form does not ask for health data; any health data in a message is processed on the basis of explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), only to answer the message
- Purpose, legal basis and retention are stated per purpose (answering messages: voluntary and explicit consent under Infotv. 5. § (1) a), GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a), plus contract / legal obligation where they apply; server logs and spam protection: legitimate interest)
- The contact form checkbox is an explicit consent statement ("I explicitly consent to UroSystem Zrt. processing the data I give in this form, including any information about my health, …") instead of "I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy". Only the text changed: as before, the checkbox is unticked by default and the form cannot be sent without it
- New sections: transfers outside the EEA (EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, EU Standard Contractual Clauses), data stored on the device (cache, local storage reading position, session storage intro screen), data subject rights and how to exercise them, complaint to the Hungarian authority (NAIH) and court remedy, no automated decision-making, changes to the Policy
- Data breach section also states the 72-hour notification to the authority
- Fixed "app. urodapter.com" spacing and "natural security" → "national security"

## Cookie Policy Changes

- Fixed "Webshop" / "purchasing goods" references to "Webpage" / "contact" in Terms and Definitions
- Removed redundant preamble (replaced with cross-reference to Privacy Policy)
- States that **no cookies** are used; the cache, local storage and session storage the app does use are not cookie-based and are listed in the Privacy Policy (no analytics)
