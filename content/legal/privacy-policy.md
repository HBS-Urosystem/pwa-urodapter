## 1. Whose data is being processed?

**Every visitor:** when you open the Webpage, your browser sends technical data (such as your IP address) to the server that delivers the pages. See section 2.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

### Answering your message sent through the contact form

- **Purpose:** to answer your enquiry or request for information.
- **Legal basis:** your voluntary and explicit consent: Section 5(1)(a) of Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 (Infotv.), Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, and for any health data in your message Article 9(2)(a) GDPR.

Before you give consent, this Policy is available to you through the link next to the checkbox. Consent is voluntary; without it the contact form cannot be sent.
- Where your message relates to a contract, or asks us to take steps before entering into a contract, the legal basis is that contract or those steps (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Where a law requires us to keep the data, the legal basis is that legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### Operating and securing the Webpage (server logs, spam protection)

- **Purpose:** to deliver the pages, to keep the Webpage and the contact form secure and free of spam, and to investigate technical errors.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in a secure, working Webpage (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this processing (section 11).

## 9. Data stored on your device

The Webpage stores the following in your browser’s storage. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is needed for functions you use, so we do not ask for consent to store it.

- **Offline copy (cache storage):** when you install or use this site as a Progressive Web App (PWA), your browser stores the app’s pages, fonts and media (such as instruction videos) so they work offline.
- **Reading position (local storage):** which branch you opened last (instillation by a clinician or self-instillation at home), which clinician instructions you opened last (female or male patients) and their last step, and which self-instillation approach and step you opened last. The Webpage uses these to return you to where you left off.
- **Intro screen (session storage):** whether the intro screen was already shown in this browser tab. It is deleted when you close the tab.

If other people use the same browser, they may see where you left off. You can delete this data at any time in your browser or device settings (site data for app.urodapter.com), or by uninstalling the app.

## Data protection

The Data Controller makes every effort (that can be expected rationally) so that the principles of the safety of data can prevail. This can be summarized in the following way.

1. **Availability** — Those who have the right to access the necessary data are able to do so at any time.
2. **Confidentiality** — Every piece of data is available only for those who are authorized to control and process it.
3. **Authenticity and Integrity** — During the process of data storing and controlling the data itself remains unchanged.

While the data is being stored, the Data Controller performs every measure of protection and precaution (that can be expected rationally) so that the data shall be protected against stealing, unauthorized accessing, becoming damaged, corrupted, deleted, destroyed and published (regardless of a result of an intentional or unintentional act), failures of electronic or any other forms (including natural disasters).

## Data breach and its handling

In case of a personal data breach, the Data Controller notifies the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the persons involved. In case of data breach of high risk, the Data Controller informs the persons who are involved (due to their rights) with no delay.

The Data Controller need not inform the persons who are involved if:

- the Data Controller performed actions which made the data affected by the breach incomprehensible (e.g. by using encryption on the data);
- informing the persons who are involved would require actions of unproportional effort (in this case information shall be provided in another form, e.g. publishing);
- due to the actions performed by the Data Controller the high risk does not exist anymore.
